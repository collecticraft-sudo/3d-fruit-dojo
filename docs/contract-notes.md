Do not rewrite or delete other people's entries. The Integrator resolves entries and marks them.

Rules (architecture.md section 14.3):

1. Implement to the contract as written unless an entry says it blocks you.
2. Purely additive fields (a new optional property, an extra payload field) need no approval but must be logged.
3. Only the Integrator edits `public/js/shared/contracts.js`, after a logged, accepted request.

### [2026-09-30] game: interpretations where the documents are ambiguous or disagree
- Kind: question
- Contract reference: architecture 7.3, 7.4, 7.6, A-23; design 2.4, 3.4, 4.5, 5.4, 9.3, 9.12
- What differs / what is needed (each item is implemented as stated and covered by a test; the Integrator may overrule any of them):
  1. **reduceMotion floor.** Design 9.12 says Freeze is "unchanged" and only the juice slow-motions get a minimum scale of 0.5; contracts.js `GameOptions` and architecture 7.3 raise the minimum scale to 0.5 for every source. Implemented as architecture 7.3: with `reduceMotion` the effective scale never goes below 0.5, Freeze included, and hit-stop is skipped.
  2. **Arcade ending.** A-23 says "freeze (arcade)" and also "300 ms freeze + 1000 ms". Implemented as the design (7.2): the world is frozen (timeScale 0) for the first 300 ms after `timeUp`, then runs again until `over` at 1300 ms; segments are ignored during the whole ending.
  3. **Combo slow-motion cooldown.** The shared 3 s cooldown covers combo4, combo7 and nearMiss (the first three rows of design table 9.3). The near-miss EVENT (popup, sting) has its own 2 s rule; its slow-motion is only started when the 3 s cooldown is clear. combo7 replaces the combo4 slow-motion of the same group regardless of the cooldown. Golden (0.4 for 350 ms) and gameOver (0.3 for 700 ms) ignore the cooldown.
  4. **SIDE formation count.** Design 2.4 lists SIDE as compatible with N = 1 or 2 and also says "N = 1 (70%) or N = 2 (30%)". Implemented: the formation is chosen among those compatible with the rolled N, then SIDE draws its own count (1 with 70%, otherwise 2, same side, second throw 250 ms later). `WaveEvent.count` reports the real count.
  5. **Extras.** Frenzy waves carry no extras and do not advance the pity counters. Breather waves carry no bomb but may carry a power-up or the golden apple. The bomb/power-up/golden roll results are drawn from sub-streams of the wave's RNG at fixed positions, so switching an extra on or off never shifts any other random value of that wave (determinism 2.8). The `hand` bias shifts only the two bands (RAIN, BREATHER, bomb, power-up, golden), not the fixed centres of PAIR, LINE, FAN and SIDE.
  6. **Frenzy timing.** The first Frenzy wave is launched at once when the medallion is cut; a regular wave that was waiting for room under the population cap is dropped (its index stays consumed). `debugSetWavesEnabled(false)` freezes the wave timer (pending launches of already released waves still happen).
  7. **`miss` events are emitted in every mode** (Arcade and Zen too, with `costsLife:false`) so that the accuracy statistic and the debug API can see them; the Presentation already keys the "Missed!" marker and the miss sound on `costsLife` / Classic. Objects removed by the side-exit or max-life safety nets also produce a `miss` (x outside 0..1920 for side exits, `costsLife:false`).
  8. **End reasons.** `RoundResult.endReason` is `'lives'` in Classic when the last life is lost (also when the last life is lost to a bomb) and `'bomb'` only with the [P2] `lethalBombs` option; `'timer'` in Arcade and Zen. `gameOver.reason` matches. `tick` events (last 10 s, once per second, re-armed when a bonus lifts the clock) are emitted in both timed modes, Zen included.
  9. **Slow bomb contact.** The design's bomb wobble on slow contact needs the sub-threshold blade position, which the Game never sees (it only gets `BladeSegment`s). The Game guarantees that slow contact never explodes a bomb; the wobble is a Presentation effect (it can use the `nearMiss` event and the blade view).
  10. **Halves cap.** The Game evicts the oldest half immediately when more than 40 exist; the 150 ms fade of design 2.6 has to be done by the renderer (a half id that disappears from `snapshot.halves` can keep drawing for 150 ms).
  11. **Snapshot details.** `snapshot.stage` is the stage of the last released wave (1 before the first wave; it changes together with the `stage` event). `combo` keeps the size and swing of the LAST group after it closed (`open:false`) until a new group opens. `stats.bestCombo` counts groups of 2 or more only (0 = none). Practice cuts report `points: 0`.
  12. **Population cap.** The uncut-fruit count includes fruit that are scheduled but not launched yet; a held wave is retried in 200 ms steps and the wave timer keeps its cadence relative to the real release time.
- Why: the two source documents leave these open; nothing here changes a field, enum or unit of contracts.js.
- Proposed change (for change-requests): none required; item 1 could be reflected in design 9.12 and item 2 in A-23.
- Affected roles: Presentation (items 7, 9, 10, 11), Integrator (documentation)
- Already implemented this way in code: yes
- Status: resolved
- Resolution (Integrator): All twelve interpretations are accepted as implemented. Items 1 (reduceMotion floor for every slow-motion source) and 2 (arcade ending: 300 ms freeze, then 1000 ms) supersede the wording of design 9.12 and architecture A-23; design 7.2/9.12 were not edited (the design document is read-only for the Integrator). The README documents Freeze, reduced motion and the round endings as implemented.

### [2026-09-30] motion Calibration wizard refinements beyond architecture 6.4
- Kind: deviation
- Contract reference: architecture.md 6.4 (step rules, sign test, scale estimate), design 12.5
- What differs / what is needed: (1) Stillness is measured on the gyro RELATIVE to a reference, not on the raw rate: step 1 uses the running mean of the hold (the bias is unknown there), steps 2 and 3 use the bias from step 1. The design numbers are unchanged (mean below 6 dps, peak below 15 dps, `abs(|a|-1) <= 0.05`); step 1 additionally caps `|mean gyro|` at 30 dps so a steady spin is not taken for a bias, and every hold also requires the accel direction to stay within 4 deg of its own mean (`stillTiltDeg`). Reason: with the alternative gyro scale (protocol D1) a 1.5 dps bias parses as 12 dps and the literal rule could never pass; slow rotations under 6 dps would otherwise pass. (2) Step 2 only starts counting a hold once the sword is at least 15 deg (`poseGateDeg`) away from pose 1, so resting at pose 1 for longer than the hold time is not reported as `bad_pose`. (3) `stepFailed('moved'|'bad_accel')` is announced only for a hold that had lasted >= 0.4 s (no nagging while the player is still moving into the pose), at most once per second; `bad_accel` is also announced after 1 s of `|a|` outside the band even if no hold ever formed. (4) Timeout or `no_data` in step 2 returns to step 1 (the integrated transition is no longer valid); timeout in step 1 or 3 restarts that step. (5) The gyro sign test also has a gravity-consistency tie-break (parallel quaternion hypotheses for sign +-1 and both scale candidates) used ONLY when the summed-rate axis test is ambiguous (|cos| <= 0.5); a data gap over 300 ms during the transition disables the estimate (warning `gyro sign undetermined`, defaults kept). (6) Without a clock and before the first sample the wizard adopts its time origin from the first sample or `poll()`.
- Why: robustness of the two-pose method for real hands and both gyro scale hypotheses; every rule is a separate constant. UNVERIFIED-ON-HARDWARE (HW-7, UOH-6, UOH-7).
- Proposed change (for change-requests): none; the text of 6.4 can absorb these points.
- Affected roles: Presentation (calibration screen messages: `moved`, `bad_pose`, `bad_accel`, `timeout`, `no_data` as in 6.4), Integrator
- Already implemented this way in code: yes
- Status: resolved
- Resolution (Integrator): Accepted. The wizard is exercised end to end by the integration tests: real parser + real pipeline for all six mounts, both sides, mirrored gyro and the 0.0075 scale (`test/app/app.test.js` `?simcal=1` cases and `test/e2e/input.test.js`), with the frame within 3 degrees of the simulator truth. UNVERIFIED-ON-HARDWARE: real hand tremor with a real sword (HW-7).
